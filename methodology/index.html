Methodology

Six stages. Six artifacts.

Each stage of the SourceDeck workflow produces a concrete output your team can use, cite, or export.

01
Intake
Paste a SAM.gov URL or upload solicitation text. SourceDeck normalizes the package — synopsis, attachments, amendments — into one workspace record.
02
Score
Bid/no-bid score 0–100. Dimensions: NAICS/PSC fit, set-aside eligibility, past performance, agency history, capacity vs period of performance, deadline runway. Written rationale per dimension.
03
Matrix
Section L vs Section M extraction. Every requirement mapped to a response location, owner, and evidence artifact. Exportable to Word with traceability preserved.
04
Draft
AI-generated proposal sections grounded in your past-performance library. Compliance matrix stays linked as the draft evolves. All AI drafts require human review before submission.
05
Review
Reviewer notes flag assumptions, missing evidence, and FAR references that require verification. Color-team reviews assigned with calendar deadlines.
06
Audit
Bid decisions, compliance matrix edits, and draft versions are logged with timestamps. Who decided what, and why, is preserved for bid review and lessons learned.
