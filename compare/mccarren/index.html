SourceDeck vs McCarren

SourceDeck vs McCarren

McCarren (AI capture / proposal). AI tool for federal capture and proposal teams.

How SourceDeck differs

McCarren is in the same neighborhood as SourceDeck. The differentiation is shape: SourceDeck leads with the deck artifact — a single-page, sourced, citable summary that makes the bid/no-bid call defensible — before any drafting begins.

Where each fits best

Both are valid. Pick the one whose primary artifact matches how your team actually decides to bid. SourceDeck is built around the source-backed deck; the compliance matrix and proposal draft fall out of it.

What SourceDeck produces

One sourced deck per opportunity, with everything that follows linked from it.

The SourceDeck artifact for a single opportunity contains: a sourced summary with citations to the solicitation, a bid/no-bid score with written rationale, a compliance matrix mapping Section L to Section M, a past-performance match against your library, a capture-action plan with FAR-aware contact posture, and a proposal-section draft starting from the matrix. Honest framing

This page is not a takedown of McCarren. They serve real customers and most of those customers chose them for good reasons. SourceDeck is positioned for small and mid-sized GovCon teams that want a practical, source-backed operating deck per opportunity — with the workflow ahead of the proposal as the first-class thing, not the proposal alone or the data lake alone.
