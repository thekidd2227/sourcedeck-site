Invoice eligibility

Invoice billing is available for verified businesses. Submit your PO and business details for review. After approval, we issue a branded Stripe invoice. Service begins only after the invoice is paid in full.

  • Verified businesses only. We verify company identity, SAM.gov / D-U-N-S / state registration, and billing contact before issuing an invoice.
  • Valid PO required. Include the PO issued by your procurement or finance team with your proposal approval. No PO means no invoice.
  • Business ID required. EIN, D-U-N-S, or UEI — used to verify the legal entity before an invoice is issued.
  • Activation begins after payment clears. Workspace provisioning and rollout start only once the Stripe invoice is paid in full.
