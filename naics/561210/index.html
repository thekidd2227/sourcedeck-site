NAICS 561210 — Facilities Support Services

Facilities Support Services covers operating support for client facilities — combinations of services such as janitorial, maintenance, trash disposal, security, mail, and reception.

Size standard

Small-business size standard for 561210 is typically $47M average annual receipts.

Capture-relevant notes

  • DoD installation support and VA medical-center support contracts are common.
  • Often a base-plus-options contract with high option-year exercise rates.
  • SDVOSB and HUBZone set-asides are common here.
