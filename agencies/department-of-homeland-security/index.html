Department of Homeland Security (DHS) opportunities

The Department of Homeland Security includes CBP, ICE, TSA, USCIS, FEMA, USCG, USSS, and other components. Procurement is decentralized across components, with shared contract vehicles for IT and professional services.

Procurement profile

DHS opportunities frequently require security clearances and may invoke covered telecom (Section 889) and CUI handling requirements. Component contracting offices have distinct capture cultures.

How SourceDeck helps with DHS opportunities

SourceDeck can help teams organize and evaluate opportunities from Department of Homeland Security: pull or paste an opportunity, score fit (NAICS, set-aside, agency history, capacity), build a compliance matrix from Section L & M, draft response sections, and run capture follow-up with FAR-aware contact posture. See the sample SourceDeck for the artifact this produces.

Capture-relevant notes

  • Section 889 representation is routinely required.
  • Many DHS task orders flow through EAGLE NextGen or other component-specific vehicles; vehicle holders see most action.
  • SourceDeck’s stakeholder graph treats DHS components as separate buying constellations.
