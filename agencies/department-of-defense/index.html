Department of Defense (DoD) opportunities

The U.S. Department of Defense is the largest federal procurement organization, with sub-agencies including the Army, Navy, Air Force, Marine Corps, Defense Logistics Agency (DLA), Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA), and many others.

Procurement profile

DoD opportunities span IT services, professional services, R&D, construction, logistics, and weapons systems. NAICS coverage is broad; set-aside use varies by sub-agency. Many DoD opportunities require security clearances or CMMC posture.

How SourceDeck helps with DoD opportunities

SourceDeck can help teams organize and evaluate opportunities from Department of Defense: pull or paste an opportunity, score fit (NAICS, set-aside, agency history, capacity), build a compliance matrix from Section L & M, draft response sections, and run capture follow-up with FAR-aware contact posture. See the sample SourceDeck for the artifact this produces.

Capture-relevant notes

  • Sub-agencies typically have separate contracting offices; capture posture differs by office.
  • Q&A and amendment cadence is heavy; SourceDeck’s SLA Watcher tracks it per opportunity.
  • Past-performance with the same sub-agency is heavily weighted in evaluation.
