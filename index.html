The six-stage workflow

Each stage is a concrete artifact, not a dashboard widget.

Source-backed outputs. Citations on every claim. FAR-aware capture posture.

📥
01 · Opportunity Intake
Pull or paste SAM.gov opportunities. Sources Sought, RFI, RFP, RFQ, IDIQ task orders, BPA call orders. Attach the synopsis and the solicitation package.
⚖
02 · Qualification
Bid/no-bid score by NAICS fit, PSC, agency history, set-aside eligibility, contract type, deadline runway, and past-performance match.
📄
03 · Source Deck
A one-page sourced summary: agency, scope, evaluation factors, period of performance, ceiling, set-aside, why this is a fit, risks, decision notes — with citations to the source document.
☑
04 · Compliance Matrix
Section L vs Section M extraction. Every requirement mapped to a response location, an owner, and an evidence artifact. Exportable to Word or your proposal tool.
🔗
05 · Capture Actions
Stakeholder graph of CO/COR/PCO/Small Business Specialist, agency program leads, prior incumbents, likely teaming partners. Next-step assignments with FAR-aware contact windows.
📝
06 · Proposal Drafting
Draft response sections grounded in your past-performance library. Compliance matrix stays linked to the draft. Follow-up reminders inside response windows.

Sample workflow

From a SAM.gov URL to a defensible bid in seven stages.

No demo video required — the artifact is the demo. Each card below maps to a real workflow stage. The full worked example is on the sample SourceDeck page.

01
Paste or import a SAM.gov opportunity
Solicitation, attachments, amendments — normalized into one workspace record.
02
Extract requirements and deadlines
Section L instructions, Section M evaluation factors, Q&A window, submission deadline — all parsed.
03
Score fit (NAICS, set-aside, agency, timing, past performance)
Bid/no-bid score 0–100 with written rationale per dimension.
04
Generate a source-backed capture deck
One-page sourced summary — agency, scope, evaluation factors, ceiling, set-aside, decision notes — with citations to the source document.
05
Build a compliance matrix starter
Section L ↔ Section M mapped, with response location, owner, and evidence per requirement. Exports to Word with traceability preserved.
06
Draft proposal sections
Response sections grounded in your past-performance library. Compliance matrix stays linked as the draft evolves.
07
Assign capture actions and follow-up
Stakeholder graph with FAR-aware contact posture. Q&A windows, amendment dates, and color-team review dates on a tracked calendar.
