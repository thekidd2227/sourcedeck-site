Past-performance write-up template

A reusable structure for CPARS-grade references

What to include

  • Customer (agency / department)
  • Contract number
  • Period of performance
  • Total contract value
  • Scope summary (3–5 sentences)
  • Relevance to current opportunity (explicit map)
  • Outcomes / results (with metrics where defensible)
  • CPARS rating if available
  • Customer point of contact (current, with permission to contact)

What to leave out

Marketing language. Logos. Anything you cannot defend in a phone call with the contracting officer. Past performance evaluators check references; do not list a contract whose POC will say something different from what you wrote.
