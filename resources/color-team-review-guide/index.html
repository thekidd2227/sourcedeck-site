Color team review guide

Pink, Red, Gold — what each one is actually for

Pink team

Earliest formal review. Read for strategy, win themes, and compliance against Section L. The proposal does not need to be polished — it needs to be on the right track.

Red team

Mid-stage review. Read for evaluator response: would a Section M evaluator score this favorably? Bring people who can simulate the evaluator’s perspective.

Gold team

Final review. Compliance, polish, and the “does this go out the door” sign-off. Catch the page-limit and font-size issues that get a proposal thrown out before evaluation.
