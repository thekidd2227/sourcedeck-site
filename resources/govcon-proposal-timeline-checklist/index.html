GovCon proposal timeline checklist

A working timeline you can run

D-30 to D-25

Solicitation in. Bid/no-bid decision with written rationale. Compliance matrix starter. Pink-team kickoff scheduled.

D-24 to D-15

Q&A questions submitted through the official mechanism. Capture actions executed (FAR-aware). Past-performance references confirmed. Pink team review.

D-14 to D-7

Section drafts complete. Red team review. Pricing locked. Reps and certs current.

D-6 to D-2

Gold team review. Compliance check against Section L and matrix. Production package prepared.

D-1 to submission

Final compliance walk. Submit. Confirm receipt. Capture the lessons-learned for the next pursuit.
