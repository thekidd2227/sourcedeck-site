Bid/no-bid checklist

Decide in 30 minutes, with a written rationale

Eligibility (must be yes)

  • Are we eligible for the set-aside?
  • Do we hold the required NAICS at a size standard the solicitation accepts?
  • Can we meet any required certifications, clearances, or location requirements?

Capability (must be mostly yes)

  • Do we have past performance the agency will recognize as relevant?
  • Can we staff the work within the period of performance?
  • Do we have the security posture the solicitation requires?

Capacity (must be honest)

  • Do we have B&P budget to write a competitive proposal?
  • Do we have a proposal lead with bandwidth?
  • Can we make the deadline without breaking other commitments?

Strategy (must be deliberate)

  • Do we have a story for why we win this specific contract?
  • Is the agency one we want to be on the awards list of?
  • Does the contract type and ceiling justify the pursuit cost?
