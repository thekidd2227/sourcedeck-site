Agents

Four agent groups. One capture workflow.

Each group runs a specific stage of the GovCon capture process and produces a concrete artifact your team can use.

Discover
Opportunity discovery
Pulls SAM.gov opportunities matching your NAICS, set-aside, and agency filters. Normalizes the solicitation package into one workspace record.
Qualify
Bid/no-bid scoring
Scores fit 0–100 across NAICS, set-aside eligibility, past performance, agency history, capacity, and deadline runway. Produces a written rationale.
Propose
Compliance & drafting
Extracts Section L/M requirements, builds the compliance matrix, and generates proposal sections grounded in your past-performance library. All AI drafts require human review.
Track
Capture follow-up
Assigns Q&A windows, amendment dates, stakeholder research tasks, and capture actions. FAR-aware contact posture. No outreach recommendations during restricted communication windows.
