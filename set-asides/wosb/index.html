Set-aside

WOSB / EDWOSB — Women-Owned and Economically Disadvantaged WOSB

WOSB set-asides reserve contracts for women-owned small businesses; EDWOSB is the economically-disadvantaged sub-tier with stricter financial-disadvantage thresholds.

Certification & eligibility

Self-certification was previously allowed; the SBA has tightened verification — certification through SBA or an approved third-party certifier is now required for WOSB / EDWOSB set-asides in most NAICS codes.

Capture-relevant notes

  • WOSB / EDWOSB eligibility is NAICS-specific — not every NAICS code is WOSB-eligible.
  • The SBA WOSB Federal Contracting Program lists eligible NAICS codes annually.
  • Certification renewal cycles must be tracked.

Common ground

  • Federal civilian agencies
  • Many DoD professional services and IT services NAICS codes
  • WOSB sole-source authority within program thresholds
