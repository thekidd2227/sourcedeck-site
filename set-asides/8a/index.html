8(a) Business Development Program

The SBA 8(a) Business Development Program is a nine-year program for small businesses owned and controlled by socially and economically disadvantaged individuals. Participation includes sole-source authority up to certain thresholds and competitive 8(a) set-asides.

Certification & eligibility

8(a) certification is granted by the SBA and renewed during the nine-year participation window. Sole-source 8(a) thresholds and the rules of the program are governed by 13 CFR 124.

Capture-relevant notes

  • Sole-source authority — up to certain thresholds — is one of the strongest tools in the program.
  • Joint ventures with non-8(a) firms require an SBA-approved JV agreement.
  • Mentor-protégé relationships under the SBA program are common.

Common ground

  • DoD, GSA, VA, USDA, and most civilian agencies
  • Many small to mid-sized professional-services contracts
  • Sole-source 8(a) awards within program thresholds
