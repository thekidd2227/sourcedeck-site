Set-aside

SDVOSB — Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business

SDVOSB set-asides reserve federal contracts for small businesses 51% owned and controlled by one or more service-disabled veterans, with the day-to-day management and long-term decisions controlled by service-disabled veterans.

Certification & eligibility

Verification through VetCert (formerly the SBA / CVE program) is required for SDVOSB set-aside eligibility on most agencies. The Department of Veterans Affairs operates the strongest SDVOSB set-aside posture under the Vets First Contracting Program (38 CFR 74).

Capture-relevant notes

  • SDVOSB self-performance rules apply (FAR 52.219-14): typically 50% of the cost of personnel must be on SDVOSB-owned firms.
  • Joint ventures with non-SDVOSB primes must follow the SBA mentor-protégé or joint-venture rules.
  • VetCert verification is current and time-bounded; renew before expiration to remain eligible.

Common ground

  • DoD, VA, GSA, DHS, HHS
  • Many DoD installation-support and VA OIT opportunities
  • SDVOSB total set-aside contracts under FAR 19.14
